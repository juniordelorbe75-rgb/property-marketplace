## 2. AEI member brokerage pilot

**Why:** A small opt-in pilot can establish inventory while a national agreement is
being discussed. Select agencies across Distrito Nacional, Santiago, Puerto Plata,

## Required answers before activation

1. Which listings and territories are included, and who owns the supplied content?
2. May the marketplace commercially display descriptions, prices, photos, and contacts?
3. What exact attribution and link-back are required?
4. How quickly must updates, withdrawals, and takedowns appear?
5. Can data be cached, normalized, translated, indexed, and retained for audit purposes?
6. Where should buyer leads go, and may the marketplace store those conversations?
7. What happens to listing data and photos when the agreement ends?
